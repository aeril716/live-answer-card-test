LIVE ANSWER CARD

LEAD PICK

A prospect asks a technical question. Before the rep can stall, the answer is on a teammate's screen.

2sRep speaks it. The call never stalls.
6sRep already said "let me check."
Slow isn't "worse" here — slow means the thing doesn't exist.
GOAL
  • Answer lands before the rep stalls
  • Keep the call's momentum alive
  • Never guess — silence over errors
TOOLS
  • Cerebras — real-time classify + generate
  • ElevenLabs — live transcription, both sides
  • RAG over our product docs
FINISHED PRODUCT
  • Live keyword card on a second screen
  • Tap a keyword → the source detail
  • Stays empty on small talk / low confidence
LIMITS & RISKS
  • The trigger classifier is the real work
  • Needs 2 screens + a rehearsed role-play
  • Venue noise degrades transcription

ASK IN YOUR LANGUAGE

FALLBACK

Upload an English lease. Ask in your own language. Hear the answer back in it, clause highlighted.

Korean
Mandarin
Spanish
Not a translator — Google Translate hands you a translated lease; you still don't know whether to sign it.
GOAL
  • Answer "is this safe to sign?"
  • Voice-first for non-native readers
  • Trust via clause-level grounding
TOOLS
  • ElevenLabs — multilingual speech in and out
  • Cerebras — fast grounded answers
  • RAG over one doc type: leases
FINISHED PRODUCT
  • Ask by voice in any language
  • Spoken answer + clause highlighted
  • Risky-clause check on request
LIMITS & RISKS
  • Quality drops at the last translation hop
  • One doc type only, or 12 hours vanish
  • "Isn't this just RAG?" needs an answer

SENIOR DOCUMENT ASSISTANT

FALLBACK

Insurance policies, hospital forms — snap a photo, ask out loud, get an answer out loud.

Same build as Idea 2, different user. Voice isn't decoration here — it's the only usable interface.
GOAL
  • Paperwork seniors can just ask
  • Voice as the only interface needed
  • Every answer shows its source
TOOLS
  • Same stack as Idea 2
  • Plus OCR for photographed forms
  • MiniMax optional: a short clip
FINISHED PRODUCT
  • Snap a photo, ask out loud
  • Spoken answer + source passage
  • Pivots ready: meds, scam calls
LIMITS & RISKS
  • OCR on phone photos is the weak point
  • Sample and public forms only
  • Least differentiated of the three
