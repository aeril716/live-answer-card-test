HOW IT WORKS

Four small decisions, running in under three seconds.

1

Listen

Both sides of the call are transcribed live. Nothing waits on the rep to type anything.

2

Decide

Small talk, pricing, and scheduling never fire the card. Only real technical questions do.

3

Answer

Grounded retrieval over your product docs — never a guess, always a cited source.

4

Show — or stay quiet

Confident enough, the card renders. Not confident enough, the screen stays deliberately empty.

THE DECISION LAYER

A wrong keyword is worse than a blank screen.

Answering is the easy half. Deciding when to say nothing at all is what keeps a rep trusting the screen enough to glance at it mid-call.

  • Small talkstays off the screen, on purpose.
  • Pricingis the rep's conversation to have, not ours to interrupt.
  • Low confidencenever becomes a guess dressed up as an answer.
